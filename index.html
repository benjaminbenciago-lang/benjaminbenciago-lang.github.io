Axis Read and Earn

Read and publish web novels, chapter by chapter

Axis Read and Earn is a free reading app where authors post their own novels and readers follow along, leave comments and pick up exactly where they stopped.

Open the app

For readers

Reading is free. Create a free account to comment, save your place in every book, and continue on any phone. Chapters open in a clean reader with an auto-scroll mode for hands-free reading.

For authors

Create an account, add your book with a cover and a description, and publish chapters when you are ready. You can edit or remove your chapters at any time. You see how many readers viewed your chapters each month.

When ads in the app earn money, 40% goes to authors and 60% keeps the app running. The authors' share is divided by how many readers viewed each author's chapters that month. Only post work you wrote yourself.

How it works

  1. Open the app and create a free account.
  2. Readers pick a book in Explore and start reading.
  3. Authors open the Write tab to add a book and publish chapters.
  4. Comics and more features are planned for later.

Privacy and terms

Last updated: September 20, 2026.

What we store

Your email address, display name, the chapter you last read in each book, your comments, and the books and chapters you publish. To count chapter views we also store an anonymous random code on your device. It is not linked to your name.

Ads and cookies

This site and app use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technology to show and measure ads. You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and manage your ad settings at adssettings.google.com.

Your content

Authors keep the rights to what they publish. By publishing, you allow the app to display your book and chapters to readers. Only post original work that you have the right to share. Content that breaks the law or infringes someone else's rights may be removed.

Ad revenue share

When ads earn money, 40% goes to authors and 60% to the app. The authors' 40% is divided by reader views of each author's chapters in that month, and an author's own views do not count. Amounts are estimates until the month is closed, and authors are paid after the ad network pays the app. Payment timing and method are agreed with the app owner.

Questions and requests

Signed-in users can send a message with the Help form in the Me tab of the app, including requests about their data.
